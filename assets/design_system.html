Contextual Usage Rules
  • Text main must always target `#1C1C1C` on `#FFFFFF` and `#EAEAE5` to preserve full AAA contrast rating.
  • Interactive accent signals (clicks, tabs) must leverage `#FF3300` for primary actions.
  • Hover feedback states must execute using scale shifts and glow offsets instead of changing background opacity.
AA / AAA Contrast Target Map
  • Canvas bg (#EAEAE5) to Charcoal Text (#1C1C1C) -> Ratio: ~12.2:1 (Passed AAA)
  • Canvas bg (#EAEAE5) to Pure Swatch (#FFFFFF) -> Ratio: ~1.2:1 (Decorative Only)
  • Aura Coral bg (#FF3300) to white text -> Ratio: ~3.8:1 (Passed AA Large)
